🎓 Viva & Lab Evaluation Guidance

Desktop Simulations vs Unity Engine:

NeuralVerse features real in-app HTML Canvas simulations for conceptual demonstration and rapid experimentation. Full 3D rendering and compilation require Unity Hub & Editor (LTS 2022/2023+).

Key Viva Concepts:

  • MonoBehaviour Lifecycle (Awake, Start, Update, FixedUpdate)
  • 3DOF vs 6DOF tracking in Mobile VR
  • Tabular Q-Learning vs Deep PPO RL
